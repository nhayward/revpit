## Winners

##### THE RENAISSANCE OF DARK MAGICIANS - Anna Baldwin

YA Fantasy

**Editor:** Allison Alexander

In their search for power, two teenage boys join a secret society for dark magicians where they must survive deadly trials, powerful competition, and a terrifying eldritch god that hunts them from the shadows.

##### FELICITY PURN BECOMES THE FALL - Kirra Joan Herter

MG Fantasy

**Editor:** Joel Brigham

Gifted with the powers of the fall, a young girl runs away to the McElroy School for Seasons, where she and her seasonally-gifted new friends must defeat an evil weatherman.

##### WITH DEATH AND DANCE - Rakan Khashman

Adult Fantasy

**Editor:** Raquel Brown

An honest liar, a gentle brute and a city where everyone wears a mask.

##### MYTH, MUSE, MAGICIAN - Nadine Flint

Adult Fantasy

**Editor:** Jeni Chappelle

An anxious, magic-cursed illusionist and a claustrophobic escape artist are star-crossed lovers who must team up to win their freedom from their rival crime families. 

##### THE GRAVITY OF HOPE - Ivy Bard

Adult Sci-Fi

**Editor:** Miranda Darrow

When a dying deserter military engineer and his ex-lieutenant amongst hundreds of others are sent from the last human city on Titan back to the dead planet Earth to compete for the last seat on a coloniser ship to another star system, the two must set aside their past feelings and grievances and team up if they want to get back to Titan alive. But only the first one who sets foot on Titan's soil can win a chance at a real future.

##### METAL BONES, PLASTIC HEART - Trinity Pierce

YA Sci-Fi/Spec. Fiction

**Editor:** Dana Draper

Lonely hacker Renaya must help a kind android boy recover his memories so she can solve her father's murder—before those behind it get to her first.

##### HAZEL DAVIES'S GOOD GOODBYE - Elizabeth Droppers

Adult Contemporary

**Editor:** Nicole Frail

A neurodivergent entrepreneur impulsively decides to sell her coffee shop to avoid grief over a friend’s impending move, only to realize long-dormant feelings from college remain and the shop is in peril of falling under possession of her creepy landlord. Hazel must face the compounding costs of avoiding hard conversations or risk losing all she holds dear.

##### HOW TO HANDLE A HAUNTING - Jude Atwood

MG Horror

**Editor:** Kala Godin

After he discovers a hidden cache of century-old ghost-hunting equipment, a twelve-year-old genius goes into business with his two best friends and confronts dangers both supernatural and personal.

##### REBIRTH OF THE CELESTIAL PRINCESS - Wera Niyom

YA Fantasy

**Editor:** Tiffany Grimes

After her father’s death, Fa Ying enrolls at a magic academy for fairies, where she encounters new friends, rivals, and a genius tech developer with a secret. As grief threatens to consume her, she must choose between revenge and acceptance to prevent the kingdom’s destruction.

##### ANYWHERE BUT HERE WITH YOU - Charisma Williams

Adult Romance

**Editor:** Natasha Hanova

Determined to fix her rut, Nigerian romance author Jesutoni flies halfway across the world to her publisher’s dating show but when she’s partnered with her ex, she must pretend their past and child don’t exist, that she’s in love with him as a means to keep herself on the show and win the prize money.

##### POTIONS UNINCORPORATED – Iris Andersi

Adult Cozy Fantasy

**Editor:** Carly Hayward

A curmudgeonly witch with a chronic illness must work with a cheerful thief to stand up to the greedy pharmaceutical company she works for.

##### KNOWING THE TWINS – Marta Pérez Fernández

MG Horror

**Editor:** Hannah Kate Kelley

Summer, 1920. Eight children must play 14-year-old Alistair’s dangerous games, where understanding him may be the only way to outpace him—and escape the summer alive. 

##### THE FIFTH DIMENSION OF MAGIC – Laura Efron

MG Fantasy

**Editor:** Madelyn Knecht

In a world separated by four dimensions of magic, two girls from rival worlds must defy expectations and overcome the newest threat to non-magical people or risk losing everything in the war they inadvertently ignited.

##### BEYOND THE MOON – Laurie Smith Murphy

MG Contemporary

**Editor:** Hanna R. Neier

When 12-year-old Ivy is determined to come up with a spectacular act for the talent show to prove she’s as unique as her siblings, she elicits help from an antique mannequin and a snarky grandma, since dusting antiques and watching her brother are her only talents.

##### THE MOST ENORMOUS THING –  Ree Augustine

MG Fantasy

**Editor:** Ash P.

Thinking he's lost his l'âme, his innermost self, Louie, a stone gargoyle come to life, journeys to find his faith in himself.

##### SKEWERED BY YOU - Jannat Noor

Adult Romance

**Editor:** Leah Rambadt

An ambitious woman transforms her family takeout restaurant into fine dining but she doesn't account for the infuriating charm of the broker trying to evict her.

##### UNRELIABLE VISIONS - Kristina Fluitt

Adult Romance

**Editor:** Megan Records

Lorelai has unreliable visions of the future. When she sees rival journalist Travis Blake's imminent death, she does what any rational woman would do: she pretends to date him to save his life.

##### FLATLINE – Kati Eisenhuth

Adult Thriller 

**Editor:** Demi Michelle Schwartz

When a rising star in Cardiology becomes the lead suspect in a string of murders at her hospital, she must use her medical expertise to identify the real killer before she becomes the next victim (or worse, gets fired). 

##### THE SHADOWS OF TIME - Marena Galluccio

YA Fantasy/Mystery

**Editor:** Caroline M. Tell

An anxious healer cursed by Time and a rakish Guardian of Fate must team up to hunt down a murderer during a masquerade ball hosted at an ancient, locked-down castle before they—or the country’s royal family—are next.

### \#RevPit 2026 Annual Contest Begins March 12th! 

Learn more about the [Annual Contest here](http://reviseresub.com/annual-contest).

#### 2026 Annual Contest Schedule: 

 * **February 20th:** Editors announced
 * **March 2- March 6:** \#MeetTheEditor sessions
   * Meet the Editor schedule coming soon!
 * **March 12- March 15th:** Submission window 
  * The submission window opens at **12pm EDT on March 12th and will remain open until 12pm EDT on March 15th**.
 * **March 15- March 23rd:** First Reader Round
 * **March 16- March 23rd:** \#AskEditor sessions
   * Ask Editor schedule coming soon!
 * **March 24th- April 10th:** [#10Queries](https://reviseresub.com/mini-events) sessions 
  * Editors will post #10Queries throughout the two weeks. Watch your chosen editors’ posts on [Bluesky](https://bsky.app/hashtag/RevPit?target=_blank) using \#10Queries and \#RevPit.
 * **April 13th:** Editor picks announced! 
  * Author-editor teams announced beginning at noon EDT. 
 * **April 13th-June 6th:** Author-editor teams work on revisions for 8 weeks
 * **June 8th:** Showcase goes live! 
  * Agent-ready queries and pages shared on the #RevPit website
